# Kaushik Ravi

I build spatial and computational tools that help a city read its own climate, ecology, water and civic life, and I carry each one from the field data and the models through to the software people use. A civil and environmental engineer by training, I write the computational stack myself.

🌐 **[kaushikravi.com](https://kaushikravi.com)**

### What I build
- **[Nature's Network Atlas](https://kaushikravi.com/projects/natures-network)** — a functional ecosystem atlas for South Bengaluru that reads 1,640 places and what nature, land and people need from each. I was tech and outcomes lead and built it end to end: data pipeline, ecological and connectivity models (species distribution + circuit theory), a cited causal knowledge graph, a decision engine, and a live public atlas. With Foundation for Nature's Enterprise; backed by Adavi Trust, the Zerodha Rewilding Fund and Rainmatter.
- **[Roots & Treefolio](https://kaushikravi.com/projects/roots)** — a venture I founded. Roots turns a phone photo into a tree's height, canopy, diameter, species and carbon; Treefolio maps 1.79 million Pune trees in the browser (DuckDB-WASM).
- **[SamaajData](https://kaushikravi.com/projects/samaajdata)** — a civic-action platform that ties real street-level signals to the actions worth taking, on a custom in-memory vector-tile engine rendering 1.17 million points in under 60 milliseconds. Built with Reap Benefit.

### How I work
Field data and remote sensing, into models (species distribution, landscape connectivity, computer vision, allometry), into data engineering (custom tile engines, DuckDB-WASM, knowledge-graph extraction), into full-stack and 3D/WebGL product. Every figure is labelled measured, modelled or judged, and where the data runs out, the tool says so.

### Background
Civil engineering at NIT Tiruchirappalli with a minor in energy and environmental engineering. DAAD WISE research scholar at TU Berlin, where I quantified Berlin's urban forest of 885,825 trees and its cooling effect.
