# Hi, I'm Kaushik Ravi 👋

I build **spatial and computational tools that help a city read its own climate, ecology, water and civic life**, and I carry each one from the field data and the models through to the software people use. A civil and environmental engineer by training, I write the computational stack myself.

Every figure is labelled measured, modelled or judged, and where the data runs out, the tool says so.

## Selected work

### [Nature's Network Atlas](https://kaushikravi.com/projects/natures-network)

[![Nature's Network Atlas](https://kaushikravi.com/images/nn/product/atlas_corridors.webp)](https://kaushikravi.com/projects/natures-network)

A functional ecosystem atlas for South Bengaluru that reads **1,640 places** and what nature, land and people need from each. I was tech and outcomes lead and built it end to end: the data pipeline, ecological and connectivity models (species distribution + circuit theory), a cited causal knowledge graph, a decision engine, and a live public atlas. *With Foundation for Nature's Enterprise; backed by Adavi Trust, the Zerodha Rewilding Fund and Rainmatter.*

### [Roots & Treefolio](https://kaushikravi.com/projects/roots)

[![Treefolio dashboard](https://kaushikravi.com/images/roots/dash_pune_overview.webp)](https://kaushikravi.com/projects/roots)

A venture I founded. Roots turns a phone photo into a tree's height, canopy, diameter, species and carbon; Treefolio maps **1.79 million Pune trees** in the browser, rendered client-side with DuckDB-WASM.

### [SamaajData](https://kaushikravi.com/projects/samaajdata)

[![SamaajData area intelligence](https://kaushikravi.com/images/reapbenefit/map_wide.webp)](https://kaushikravi.com/projects/samaajdata)

A civic-action platform that ties real street-level signals to the actions worth taking, on a custom in-memory vector-tile engine that renders **1.17 million points in under 60 ms**. *Built with Reap Benefit.*
